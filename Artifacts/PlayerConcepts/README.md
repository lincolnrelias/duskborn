# Player visual concepts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on 2026-09-29. Early concepts only; no player model, rig, character creator or Unity assets changed.

Inspected style references: Artifacts/HollowWarden/Reference/approved-concept.png and Artifacts/FrescoFurnace/Furnace_Preview.png, plus existing production notes. References informed the written prompts.

1. 01-hearthland-wayfarer.png — approachable rustic foundation; recommended.
2. 02-ironroot-vagabond.png — angular rugged survivor.
3. 03-duskveil-wanderer.png — lean folklore adventurer.

All directions support masculine/feminine presentation and are general-purpose, not classes. Proposed initial creator: two body presets sharing skeleton layout and similar joint placement, several head/hair/beard presets, skin/hair colors and clothing tint. Armor needs fitting to each supported body preset; shared skeleton alone does not ensure fit. Avoid height/limb sliders initially. Keep head/chest/hands/legs/feet equipment modular, hide covered body regions, and swap or hide hair beneath helmets. Weapons and held items use hand attachment points.

Production should simplify the excessive small facets and surface wear in these illustrations to broad planes consistent with existing assets. Generated marginal slogans and logos are incidental, not proposed branding or lore. Views are exploratory and may differ; reconcile before modeling. Humanoid retargeting depends on actual rig, bind pose and skin weights, not the concept silhouette. In-game readability, armor clipping and animation are unverified. Once modeled, manually inspect at the usual gameplay camera and during idle, run, crouch and attacks, with each armor/body combination.
